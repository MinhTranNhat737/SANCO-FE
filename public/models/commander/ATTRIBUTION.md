Runtime adaptations: static poses baked and meshes merged by material; models
scaled and oriented to fit a board intersection; materials tinted red/blue;
square bases, unit labels and hero badges added. Carrier stacks show miniatures
at different heights. The downloaded originals are not overwritten.

[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
applies to the ten CC0 assets. Attribution and license links for the ship are
also shown under “Nguồn mô hình 3D” on the game screen in both play modes.
